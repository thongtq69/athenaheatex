The small scale milk, yoghurt, juice combined production line

Contact
  • +86-18616619098
  • info@shjoylong.com
  • Description
  • Parameter
  • Video
This combined production line adopts the most advanced technology, it combine the water treatment system, mixing tank, emulsify tank, homogenizer, sterilizer, fermentation tank, storage tank, water chiller, air compressor, steam boiler, CIP,pipes, fittings etc as one combined production line, the system outlet can be connected directly to the filling machine. The system have feature of reasonable structure, beautiful shape, can greatly reduce the production area of space, less worker of operation, low cost of maintenance, This combined production line can produce pasteurized milk, yogurt, ice ream, fruit juice, tea beverages, etc. All electrical appliances and accessories are from international famous brand, Stainless steel structure, conform to the hygiene standard.
